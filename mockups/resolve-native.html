C · Inside Resolve: clicking Workspace → Scripts → MotionCaption opens this small window over Resolve, drawn by Resolve itself. Nothing to switch to. "Try at playhead" puts one real Text+ caption on V3, so the preview in Resolve's own viewer is exact. "Place" puts all of them. "More…" opens the full app for per-word work.
D · Resolve-style Studio: the app, laid out like Resolve's Edit page: Effects-Library-style animation bins with moving thumbnails, the viewer, an Inspector and a timeline with the same tracks as Reel_Final. SRC/TGT on the track headers pick where subtitles come from and where captions go. Send, then one click on MotionCaption in Resolve places them.
